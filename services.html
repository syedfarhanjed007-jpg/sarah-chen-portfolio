FAQ

Common questions

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary based on scope. Brand identity projects typically take 6-12 weeks, web design 4-8 weeks, and motion design 2-6 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

What's your design process like?

Every project starts with discovery — understanding your goals, audience, and vision. From there, I move through strategy, concept development, design, and refinement. You'll be involved at every stage with regular check-ins and presentations.

Do you work with startups?

Absolutely! I love working with startups and emerging brands. I offer flexible engagement models and can scale the scope to fit your budget and stage of growth.

What do I need to get started?

A clear brief is helpful but not required. Even if you only have a rough idea, I can help shape it into a concrete project scope. Just reach out and we'll figure it out together.

Do you offer ongoing design support?

Yes! After an initial project, I offer monthly retainer packages for ongoing design support. This is great for brands that need consistent creative output without hiring a full-time designer.
