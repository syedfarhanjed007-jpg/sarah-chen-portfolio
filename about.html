About Me

Designer, storyteller, and creative thinker based in San Francisco.

Hello, I'm Sarah. I've been in love with visual design since I first discovered how color, type, and space can evoke emotion and tell stories without words.

My journey started with a Fine Arts degree from RISD, followed by roles at several design studios where I honed my craft across brand identity, digital design, and motion graphics. Along the way, I discovered that the most impactful design isn't just beautiful — it's thoughtful, strategic, and deeply human.

Today, I work with forward-thinking brands and startups to create visual identities and digital experiences that resonate. I believe in the power of simplicity, the beauty of restraint, and the importance of design that serves people first.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring ceramics, hiking the Marin headlands, or hunting for the perfect pour-over coffee.
